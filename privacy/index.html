Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Grow+Change is currently in development. This Privacy Policy describes the intended handling of information as the platform is developed and may be updated as features and integrations evolve.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you choose to use, Grow+Change may collect information such as account details, profile information, application preferences, educational progress, simulated trading activity, trading journal information, achievements, community activity, and subscription status.

2. Brokerage Information

If you choose to connect a supported brokerage account, Grow+Change may receive authorized brokerage information such as account identifiers, balances, positions, orders, executions, and historical trading activity.

Brokerage information is intended to be used for features such as analytics, journaling, performance review, reconciliation, educational insights, and other user-requested functionality.

3. Brokerage Authorization

Brokerage connections may use secure authorization methods provided by the brokerage or integration provider. Users should not provide their brokerage passwords directly to Grow+Change unless a future feature explicitly and securely requires otherwise.

Grow+Change's current brokerage integration is designed for read-only access and does not place, modify, or cancel live brokerage orders.

4. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Grow+Change features.
  • Authenticate and maintain user accounts.
  • Provide trading education and simulated trading experiences.
  • Generate analytics, journals, statistics, and performance insights.
  • Track educational progress, challenges, and achievements.
  • Operate community and leaderboard features.
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse.
  • Improve platform reliability and functionality.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

5. Third-Party Services

Grow+Change may rely on third-party providers for services such as brokerage connectivity, cloud infrastructure, authentication, market data, payments, analytics, and application distribution. Information may be shared with these providers only as reasonably necessary to provide their services or comply with applicable law.

6. Sale of Personal Information

Grow+Change does not intend to sell users' personal information or brokerage information.

7. Security

Grow+Change is designed to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information being handled. However, no internet-based service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the platform, maintain records, resolve disputes, protect the service, and satisfy applicable legal obligations.

9. User Choices

Users may be able to update account information, disconnect supported third-party integrations, and request deletion of certain information, subject to applicable legal, security, and record-retention requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

Grow+Change is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use the platform under applicable law. Additional age requirements may apply to brokerage-related functionality.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Grow+Change develops. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact

Contact information for privacy, legal, and support inquiries will be added before public commercial launch.

Development draft. This Privacy Policy should receive professional legal and privacy review before Grow+Change is commercially launched.
